BEFORE ITHACA · DOWNLOAD GUIDE

1. Fastest, any computer
Open Before_Ithaca_Standalone.html in Chrome, Safari, Edge, or Firefox.

2. macOS application
Unzip Before_Ithaca_macOS_App.zip and open “Before Ithaca.app”. This app is unsigned, so macOS may require right-clicking it and choosing Open the first time.

Unzip Before_Ithaca_Odyssey_Story_Atlas.zip. It contains index.html, styles.css, app.js, local artwork, and launchers for macOS, Windows, and Linux.
